What is the primary objective of applying a wound dressing?
A. To keep the wound completely dry
B. To protect the wound from further injury and infection
C. To accelerate the inflammatory phase
D. To eliminate the need for cleaning
ANSWER: B
According to evidence-based practice, what type of environment best promotes wound healing?
A. A dry environment to prevent bacterial growth
B. A moist wound environment
C. A sealed, airtight environment
D. An environment with high oxygen exposure
ANSWER: B 
 A nurse is assessing a pressure ulcer with yellow slough and minimal exudate. Which dressing is most suitable?
A. Dry gauze
B. Hydrogel dressing
C. Alginate dressing
D. Transparent film
ANSWER: B 
When treating a highly exudative (heavy drainage) wound, which dressing type is the best choice?
A. Transparent film
B. Hydrocolloid
C. Alginate
D. Hydrogel
ANSWER: C 

What is the correct procedure for cleansing a surgical wound?
A. Clean from the dirtiest area to the cleanest area
B. Clean from the center of the wound outward
C. Use alcohol-based solutions on the granulation tissue
D. Scrub the wound vigorously
ANSWER: B 
Which of the following is considered a sign of a localized wound infection?
A. Increased serosanguineous drainage
B. Erythema, warmth, and purulent drainage
C. Presence of granulation tissue
D. Decrease in pain
ANSWER: B 
When performing a sterile dressing change, the nurse should remove the old dressing using:
A. Sterile gloves
B. Clean (non-sterile) gloves
C. Forceps
D. Bare hands
ANSWER: B 
Which type of debridement uses the body's own enzymes to break down necrotic tissue?
A. Surgical debridement
B. Autolytic debridement
C. Mechanical debridement
D. Enzymatic debridement
ANSWER: B 

What is the primary purpose of a pressure dressing?
A. To keep the wound moist
B. To promote debridement
C. To control bleeding through firm pressure
D. To absorb exudate
ANSWER: C
A wound that fails to progress through the healing stages over a 4-week period is classified as:
A. Acute
B. Chronic
C. Surgical
D. Primary intention
ANSWER: B 


 Which irrigation solution is preferred for cleansing a clean, granulating wound?
A. Hydrogen peroxide
B. Povidone-iodine
C. Normal Saline (0.9% NaCl)
D. Alcohol
ANSWER: C

Hydrocolloid dressings are most appropriate for which type of wound?
A. Deep, heavily infected wounds
B. Dry, necrotic wounds
C. Superficial to partial-thickness wounds with light to moderate exudate
D. Wounds with active arterial bleeding
ANSWER: C 
A patient has a venous leg ulcer with moderate to heavy drainage. Which dressing regimen is most appropriate?
A. Hydrogel and no compression
B. Calcium alginate and compression therapy
C. Dry gauze only
D. Transparent film only
ANSWER: B 
When should a nurse change a wound dressing?
A. Every 24 hours regardless of the type
B. Only when the patient complains of pain
C. When the dressing is saturated or soiled
D. Once a week
ANSWER: C 

What does "sanguineous" drainage look like?
A. Clear, watery plasma
B. Thick, yellow, or green
C. Pale, red, watery
D. Bright red, indicating active bleeding
ANSWER: D 

Which of the following is a contraindication for using negative pressure wound therapy (VAC)?
A. Chronic wounds
B. Malignancy in the wound
C. Pressure ulcers
D. Dehisced wounds
ANSWER: B 

What is the first step in the nursing process regarding wound care?
A. Choosing a dressing
B. Cleansing the wound
C. Assessing the wound
D. Educating the patient
ANSWER: C 
When assessing a wound, "tunneling" refers to:
A. The depth of the wound bed
B. The destruction of tissue forming a channel under the skin
C. The granulation tissue growth
D. The color of the wound edge
ANSWER: B 

Which nutrient is essential for collagen synthesis during the wound healing process?
A. Vitamin K
B. Iron
C. Vitamin C
D. Calcium
ANSWER: C 

When documenting wound care, which of the following must be included?
A. Type of dressing applied, size of wound, drainage amount, and odor
B. Cost of the dressing
C. Patient's favorite nurse
D. The patient's opinion on the wound
ANSWER: A
