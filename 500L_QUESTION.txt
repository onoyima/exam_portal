What is the recommended angle of needle insertion for an intramuscular injection?
A. 15 degrees
B. 30 degrees
C. 45 degrees
D. 90 degrees 
ANSWER: D
Which site is considered the safest for administering an intramuscular injection to an adult due to its distance from major nerves and blood vessels?
A. Dorsogluteal
B. Ventrogluteal
C. Vastus lateralis
D. Deltoid 
ANSWER: B 
What is the maximum volume of medication that can generally be safely administered in the deltoid muscle of an adult?
A. 0.5 mL
B. 1–2 mL
C. 3–4 mL
D. 5 mL
ANSWER: B

A. Dorsogluteal
B. Ventrogluteal
C. Vastus lateralis

Which needle size (gauge and length) is typically recommended for a standard adult IM injection?
A. 25 gauge, 5/8 inch
B. 22–25 gauge, 1–1.5 inch
C. 18 gauge, 2 inch
D. 27 gauge, 1/2 inch

When administering an oil-based, irritating medication, which technique should the nurse use to prevent leakage into the subcutaneous tissue?
A. Z-track method
B. Air-lock technique
C. Massage vigorously
D. Rapid injection 
ANSWER: A The Z-track method displaces the skin, sealing the medication within the muscle.
